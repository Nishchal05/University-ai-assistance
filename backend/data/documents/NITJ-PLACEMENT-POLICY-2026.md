1.INTRODUCTION
The Government of India has taken significant initiatives to promote industrial internships in technical institutes.
NEP-2020 includes four-to-eight-week and six-month internships as essential components, ensuring students are
adequately prepared for the professional world. Various model internship guidelines have also been released.
detailing procedures for establishing, managing, and facilitating internships in collaboration with industries and
organizations.
Aligned with these national objectives, Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar, considers
industrial training and internships to be integral part of its engineering curriculum. As per the B.Tech Ordinances
2023, students are required to undertake internship of 3 to 4 weeks after the 4th semester and 4 to 6 weeks after
the 6th semester, while a six-month industry internship is optionally allowed during the 7th or 8th semester.
Additionally, M.Tech students are encouraged to pursue internships ranging from six months to one year to
enhance their industrial exposure and research capabilities. This policy document outlines the institutional
framework and guidelines for internships and placement applicable to both B.Tech and M.Tech students, whether
undertaken within the institute, in collaboration with external organizations, or directly in industry.
The responsibility of securing a placement or internship through the CTP shall be a shared effort between the
students, faculty and the CTP. Students may obtain internships either independently or through the CTP. They are
encouraged to forward relevant contacts, including those from alumni or industry, to the CTP so that formal
invitations may be extended. Faculty members may also assist in identifying internship opportunities through their
academic or industry networks. In all cases, whether the internship is student-initiated or facilitated by faculty, it
is mandatory to inform the CTP. Faculty coordinators provide necessary guidance and oversight throughout the
internship process. Additionally, all internships must adhere to the terms and conditions specified in any
Memoranda of MoUs signedwith partner institutions.
